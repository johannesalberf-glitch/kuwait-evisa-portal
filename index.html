The photograph, the seal, the barcode, the QR block and the MRZ lines on the demonstration document are labelled placeholders. Nothing on it is scannable or verifiable.

Valid

A green strip carries the expiry date, the days remaining, and the Arabic line حالة التأشيرة: سارية.

Expired

Past the expiry date the strip turns red, counts the days since, and reads حالة التأشيرة: منتهية الصلاحية.

Questions about this portal

أسئلة عن هذه البوابة

Is this an official Ministry of Interior service? هل هذه خدمة رسمية؟ +

No. It is a demonstration prototype built for layout review and flow testing. It is not connected to any government system, and the emblem, the seal, the barcode, the QR block and the MRZ lines are labelled placeholders, not verifiable credentials.

Where are the records stored? أين تُحفظ السجلات؟ +

In the Supabase project connected to this build. Only demonstration records belong there: the page warns against entering real passport or personal data, and anyone who holds a share link can read its record, so keep every entry fake.

The reference returns nothing. What now? رقم المرجع لا يعطي نتيجة. ماذا أفعل؟ +

Retype the six characters exactly as they appear on the notification, letters included. If it still fails, ask the issuing office to confirm the number. A failed check changes nothing in the register.

Can I download or print the visa? هل يمكن تنزيل التأشيرة أو طباعتها؟ +

Yes. Download image saves a PNG of the document, and Print or save as PDF opens a print view with only the document on the page. Both exist so the printed layout can be reviewed, and the document itself is marked as a demo either way.

Does the QR code or barcode scan? هل يعمل الرمز الشريطي أو رمز الاستجابة السريعة؟ +

No. The QR block, the barcode and the MRZ lines are drawn illustrations for the layout, and the document says so on its face.

Who can open the staff console? من يستطيع فتح لوحة الموظفين؟ +

Anyone holding the passcode. It is a demo gate written in the page source, not a login, so the console suits test data only.

Cloud storage (how a record reaches another phone)

Records are stored in the Supabase project configured at the top of the script in index.html, in the table kuwait_visas created by supabase-schema.sql. A record saved here is readable from any phone that opens its share link, including inside Telegram.

This page ships only the public publishable key, which can read and write, so keep every record fake. The secret and service_role keys from the Supabase dashboard must never be pasted into a page like this one.
